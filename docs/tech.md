Description

PanGu transforms physical movement into a fully navigable three-dimensional performance space through hybrid RGB and depth-based vision. Instead of composing sound only along the left–right stereo axis, performers sculpt sound directly in XYZ space, mapping gestures and positions to multichannel loudspeakers and spectral processes.

Designed as both a live performance instrument and an experimental composition platform, PanGu treats space itself as a playable musical parameter—allowing artists to perform architecture, distance, and motion as sound.

Role

Hardware Design, Circuit Implementation, Product Modeling

Media
	•	122-Transducer Array Photo
	•	Radiation Pattern Testing Platform Diagram

Links

[GitHub]

Description

Dream Pillow is a 122-transducer ultrasonic phased-array system that delivers highly directional spatial audio in open air—without headphones. By exploiting the beam-forming properties of ultrasonic modulation, it creates private listening zones that exist physically in space.

To overcome the perceptual limitations of traditional HRTF-based virtual spatialization, the system integrates real-time head tracking, allowing the acoustic field to remain spatially stable as the listener moves. The result is a physically grounded form of personal, immersive sound.

Qi (ESax)

Stack

Role

Concept, Sensor Integration, PCB Design

Media
	•	Instrument Photo / Live Performance Video

Description

Qi (ESax) introduces the first electronic wind instrument to support independent left- and right-hand control as separate expressive dimensions. Through the integration of Trill sensors and motion-tracking accelerometers, performers can simultaneously manipulate pitch, timbre, and spatial effects.

This decoupled control paradigm expands the expressive bandwidth of electronic saxophone performance, enabling gestures that are physically intuitive yet sonically multidimensional.

Jazz with Li is a curated MIDI dataset focused on the structural depth of advanced jazz harmony. It includes annotated chord progressions, dense voicings, modal interchange, and functional harmonic relationships.

The dataset is designed to support machine-learning research in jazz composition, automatic harmonization, and style-transfer systems where musical semantics matter as much as sound.
